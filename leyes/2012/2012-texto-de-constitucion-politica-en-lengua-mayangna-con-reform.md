NIKARAWAHLANI RIKNI:

Tuna Niningkawa Yulni

MAYANG,

Nikarawah Pani Muihni Tanituna, King Lani Rikni yamyang balna kapat kaluduhna mayang,

Ma ising katda ki

Sara mani kaupak ma inkidian sulani balna kalbauwi aina kidi. Sau wisamni yalahwa pani sulani bitik kaluduh yalahnin kulnin lani duda kidi, dawak ma paki balna nining kulnin parasni duwi tanitdakwi aina balna kidi, dawak tannika laih talnin sip mayang, General JOSE DOLORES ESTRADA, ANDRES CASTRO dawi ENMANUEL MONGALO, balna yayamna yak, witinna kuduh, kuma paskaupak dimalyang ma sauki yak aina balna dawak miriki suku turukyang balna niningkauh bik kalbauwi minit bayayakna dai kidi.

BENJAMIN ZELEDON Sau uk muihni balna ai kawi ma sauki daknin yayamna nining kauh kalbauna kidi.

Pri Ahalni Balna Generalni, AUGUSTO C. SANDINO, Pa Muihni dawi MuihTaihyang Niningkauh batanghna Tingnita yamyang Papangni.

Kulnin dadasni duwi kalbauna RIGOBERTO LOPEZ PEREZ, witin kuduh Muihtaihyang gabamintni witnin tani tunan bahna kidi.

CARLOS FONSECA tingta yamna wayani talda kidi, Sandino tingta yamna usnit uiyang baisa nuhni kidi, Frente Sandinista de Liberación Nacional tuna bahna muihni, tanituna.

King sauni aiska pri yaknin yulni tani tunan duwi kalbauwi dadauna balna dawak minit bayayakna balna bitik kau.

Adika balna ayangnina kau

Nicaragua walanibis balna; Sahwi kalahwi, kaluduhna satni balna bitik kau; saunina kau dalani talwi ribulusan usnit uywa bitika kau; Nicaragua ahalni dawi yayalni balna bitik kau: wark muihni dawi di dahyang balna bitik kau; minit bayakyang wahnima dawi sirauni balna; minit bayakyang itangni balna; papang kau barangni kulwi muih miniknakunni yulni kalbauwi aina balna kidi; sins duna balna saunini kulwa muihni balna; dawak angdika bitik warkni yamwi minik yakwi sauni aiska tanitdaknin warkni kau paraswa balna bitik kau.

Sulani wisamni aiwa balna barangni alasna atnin yulni muihtaihyang balna niningkauh kalbauwi aiwi dawak sangninaka bik diawa muihni balna yulnina bik.

Yulni kat

Ribulusan wark yamwi minik yakna balna, dawak pa muihni yalahwa satni wisamni as, taihna lani dis yalahwa tannika as binin yulni kat, warkni yamwi anipis balna bibina karang, dawak Nicaragua muihni balna sangnika, barakgnin tani yak, papatni yalahwa atnin, dawak muih ramhnina kidi kasak palni risnipik kalana karang.

MASAUNI AISKA, TINGTAWISAMNI BTANHNAMASAUKI SULANI BIITK KALUDUHWI YALAHNIN YULNI DAWAK PRI LANI YULNI KAT

Ma takitkau la adika ukwa ki.

NIKARAWAH KINGNI KAWA SAUNI AISKA LANI RIKNI YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI KULNIN TUHNI RIKNI BALNA LA TUNA SAHYAKNA ASLAH

Lanibin 1.

Nikarawah Sauni dakwi Kingni awi duwi, Sauni tanitdakwi, Alas lani awi duwi Yalahwa kidi, pa muihni ramhnina kapat sip dadawas karang ki, dawak Nikarawah sulani kingni awi yalahwa kulnin lani tuhni rikni as ki. King sauni uk kaupak Nikarawah sangnika pasyak kulnin lani anin trai talwa awaskat, ramhnina parasni parahkau laknin trai talwa bitik kidika pa muihni sangnika niningkauh yamwa karang ki. Nikarawah walanibis bitik salahni yak duwa ki, adika ramhni balna tanit dakwi minit talna atnin.

Lanibin 2.

Nikarawah sauni daklawi Kingni awi yalahwa parasni kidi pa muihni sangnika pas yak sakki, dawak warkni yamwa ki, pa muihni bitik parasni anin tani balna munah, pri lani munah tingta pasyak kawi dawi kulnin barangni awa ki, yaka munah sangnika baraknin, pulitik dawak pa muihni tani yayamni balna bibiwa ki. Gabamint tingta parasni kidi, pa muihni balna tunan duwa ki, witinna balna kuduh, tanituna balan pri lani munah, papatni, barangni dawak minitpakwi walwi yayakwa ki, muih uk awaskat kaluduhna as yaklauwi taihwi di pakwak walwi yayakwas ki tanituna balna. Dawak bik sip karang, pa muihni bitik kaluduhwi tanituna walwi yakna atnin, ampat tani balna adika lani rikni dawak lani uk kau barangni yayamna yaka munah yamnin sip karang.

Lanibin 3.

Pri lani dawak King sauni uk balna pas yakat bik la barangni papatni as duwi yalahnin usnit uywi patni kalahnin yulni, Nicaragua walanibis kapat pranimis duwa kidi sip rumhwi dawas karang. Yaka yulni kat, muih uk kau taihnin, taihwi di yamwi King sauni uk balna manah baraknin wawalwa kidika lani bitik niningkauh mayang, dawak pa muihni balna taihna lani dawi ahni kau danin lani ninigkauh kalbabauwa balna bitik kau di

parasda ki.

Lanibin 4.

Muih bitik yamnini yulni kat, gabamint yaklauwi paraswi barangni yamwa karang, pa muihni sangnika dawi warkni baraknin yulni, yaka yulni Nikarawah ahalni, yayalni bitik sangninaka baraknin yulni di parasna karang, kaput bahang tanit dakna karang taihna lani, ahni kau danin dawak, kangwi yaknin lani balna bitik niningkauh.

Lanibin 5.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kulninna tuhni kau duwa ki, pri lani kidi, la barangni kidi, muih muihni nuhnini risnipik kalanin kidi, pulitik, pa muihni dawi sulani satni mahni kidi, sau dawi yamnini daniwan satni duwa kidi, King sauni uk karak, pri lani kat biri biri kalparasnin lani kidi, dawak sulani balna bitik pri lani kat kingni lani awi duwi yalahnin kidika.

King tingta laihnin wawalwa daknini satni mahni kalpakwi wark yamwa munah, barangni yamwa ki, pulitik kaluduhna satni mahni kidi kalpakwi wark yayamwa atnin, pa lalahnin, gabamint tingta dawi pa muhni balna bitik sangnika baraknin yulni kat, kulnin lani satni bitik kau ramhnina duduwa ki, kaunah niningkauh karang ki, angdika dakni balna, pa muihni kau taihwi diyamnin awaskat, pa muihni kulnin lani niningkauh yayamwa balna kidika.

King tingta parasni adika indian sulani bang kidika risnipik kalawa ki, dawak adika King Lani Rikni kau ramhnina, dawi yamnin duwa balna kidi bitik yus munnin ramhnina duwa ki, baisa palni laih, sara mani kaupak sulani lanina duduwa kidika ban duwi yus munwi barakna atnin, pani muihni balna alas kaluduhnin lani duduwa

kidika, dawak witinna pas kau di duduwa kidi alas tuna duwi wauhna atnin, kaput bik, pa binina kal uduh yalahwi saunina dakwi lawi yus mumunwa kidika ban duduwa atnin, dawak yamnini balna ban duwi yus munwa atnin, bitik kidika la yulwa pas munah karang. Atlantik Kus pani muihni pani binina yulni kat, Alas Yalahwa lani as yamwi dawa ki, adika Nikarawah Kingni Lani Rikni pas akat.

Di daniwan kalahnin satni bitik kidika: Gabamint dini, Alas dini, Dakni as dini, didahyang daknini as dini dawak indian pani muihni dini kidi, barangni tanit dakna karang, ahni kau danin lani dis, pa ritsni dulaunin yulni kat, dawak bitik kidika, kulninna pri lani kat, pa muihni sangninnaka barakin tingta yamna karang.

Nikarawah kidi King sauni uk karak kalpakwi tingta yamwa ki, King sauni as King sauni uk karak biri biri kalpakwi kalparaswa lani munah. Yaka bahang, King sauni uk kau kawi, gabamnit tingta yamnin, arakbus karak kanin, lalahni tani kau, sulani sangnika kau, awaskat, suts warkni yayamwa kulnin munah duwi, King sauni uk sangnika pas kanin lani kidi rumhwi dawa ki dawak yamwas karang. King sauni as dawi King sauni uk karak isninglak lani sat uk sat uk duduwarang kat, King sauni mahni kalpakwi la yayamna pasmunah kawi barangni yayamna karang, dawak King as sauna paskau, awaskat, King biri biri kakahni duwa yulni, bumba dudutni rumhnin lani balna, dawi di mahni daukalnin dini rumhnin lani balna bitik niningkauh ki; King as yaklauwi sauni walanibis as kau kakahni kalyamnin usnit uywa kat, tanit dakna karang, kaput bik, King as yaklauwi, King uk kalni dinit kau buknin lani kidi nining kau ki.

Nikarawah kidi kal irikwa ki Sau Wisamni Yalahwa Pani Bitik Kalpakna Ramhnina kulnin lani tuhni rikni ukna balna pasyakat, kidika kuduh, Kingnina lani parsni duwa munah, talwi yakwi ayangnina rawasna ki. Nikarawah, Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus kingni sauni balna karak kalpakwi yalahnin kidi yaksihni talwa ki, dawak trai talwa ki, mani as Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus kingni sauni balna kidi sau aslah kapat yalalahna atnin lani

watwi dulauna atnin.

YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI II

GABAMINT SAUNI PARASNINI YULNI

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA ASLAH

Lanibin 6.

Nikarawah kuduh gabamint as parasni alas duwi, pri ki, sauni daklawi tanit dakwi duwi, sauni parasni uduhna aslah ki, dawi sip sahwi kalahwas ki.

Lanibin 7.

Nikarawah kuduh pa muihni balna karak kalpakwi gabamintni tunan duwa ki, pa muihni balna tingta, pasyak kawi tunana duduwa ki.Gabamint tingni rikni balna ki: La Binin Parasni, Gabamnit Tingta Wauhnin Parasni, Gabamnit Lani Laihwi Yamnin Parasni, dawi Iliksan Tunan Wauhnin Parasni.

Lanibin 8.

Nikarawah pani muihni balna kuduh sulani satni mahni ki, dawak Abya Yala papus sauni pasyak sak ki.

Lanibin 9.

Nikarawah laih barangni tanit dakwa ki, Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus kingni sauni balna kidi kalpakwi aslah yalahna atnin, yaka yulni Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus sauni balna paskau tingta balna dawak lalahni baraknin tingta yamnin dawi biri biri kalparsnin kulnin lani kidi paraswi tanitkau dulauwi kiwa ki, kaput bik, sauni pisni akat, pri lani duwi yalahnin lani kidi barangni diparaswa ki.

Nikarawah dukih kalawi Sau wisamni yalahwa pani aiska dawi kuma paskau kalansah sauni walanibis balna bitik kidika kalpakwi aslah yalahna atnin, Bolívar dawi Sandino kulnin lani duduwa dai munah.

Yaka bahang, Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus dawak Sau wisamni yalahwa pani aiska sauni kingni balna karak kalpakwi adika warkni yamnin yulni kat, kaluduhna satni balna walwi bibina karang, dawak bik walwi yayakna karang.

Kulnin lani tuhni rikni adika tingnita yamni kat, King sauni uk uk balna karak kalpakwi yulbauwi la yayamwarang munah yayamna karang ki.

Lanibin 10.

King sauni daklana aiska adika kuduh libitna sakki, karibi kumani dawi pasipik kumani, kaput bik Honduras dawi Costa Rica Sauni daklana papus yakat. Nikarawah sauni kingni, parasni dawak ramhni kidi bayakwa ki, kumani kung kalansah nunuhni dawi bikikisni balna, kaput bik, kaumah yaihnitkau bang balna kidika bik, kaput bik paskau was bang balna kidika bik, kuma pisni bik, yaihnit kau sauni bik, kuma dinit sauni bik, lalahni barakin tingnita yamnin pisni bik, dawak ma kau wing kira pisni daklana bik, bitik kidika, King sauni mahni kaluduhwi la yayamna yakat lani awi duwa ki.

Nikarawah kingni sauni adika king sauni uk kau karak risnipik lani kalana karang, angdika balna pri lani munah kulnin lani barangni ana balna kidika manah, dawak bik Nikarawah kingni lani rikni munah, dawi dawi king mahni kaluduhwi la yayamna balna balna kidika manah. Kaput bahan, angdika lani balna, king sauni uk balna

rawaswasna karang bik, Nikarawah pas kau kawi rawaswas balna kalaih risnipik kalawas karang.

Lanibin 11.

Ispayul tuni kidika king sauni pas akat tuni palni kapat yus munna karang. Nikarawah Atlantik Kus sauni kau sulani tuni uk yuyulwa balna kidika bik, la kau ais yulwarang yakat, tuni palni kapat yus munna karang.

Lanibin 12.

Manawah pani adika king sauni Kapitalni ki, dawak King Tingni Rikni Parasni balna anipis rikni duwarang ki. Tannika uk as kalahwarang laihtak, pa uk kau dunin sip karang, adika king sauni daklana pas akat.

Lanibin 13.

King sauni daklana wayani balna kuduh: King Sauni Lanwana, Sauasna dawak Sauasna wayani, dawak la ukna munah yamna ki, dawak bik ampat yus munnin bik lani ana ki.

Lanibin 14.

King sauni parasni adika alas sutsni kapat as diski.

YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI III

NIKARAWAH WALANIBIS SULANI KIDIKA

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA ASLAH

Lanibin 15.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi sipki sulani atnin, awaskat sulani kalna atnin.

Lanibin 16.

Sulani balna ki:

1) Kingni sauni pas akat batanhna balna kidi. Kidi pasyak kakawas ki, king sauni uk balna, kingni warkni yamwa bang walanibis balna, Kin sauni uk muihni balna aninapis tingnita yulni aiwi kakana balna kidika, kaput bik, kingnina yaklauwi Nikarawah sauni kau warkni yamnin sisihna balna kidika, ban bik, Nicaragua walanibis kalnin yulni yuyulwarang laihta kat, sip karang ki.

2) Walabis papangni kidi awaskat itangni kidika Nikarawah walanibis kat.

3) Sau uk kau batanhna muihni bin kidi papangni awaskat itangni kidika tanitkau Nikarawah walanibis dai kat, kauna kidika bik, kurihni muihbarak laihwarang mani Nikarawah walanibis kalnin yulwarang kat.

4) Nikarawah sauni pas akat, muihbinina dini almuk dis kat walwi yayakna balna kidika, trabil as bik dis karang ki, dini almuk kalahwarang mani lani uk yamnin mani kaiwarang balna bik.

5) Batanhna muihni bin dini almuk balna king sauni uk muihni balna karang bik, Nikarawah plinni awaskat kuringnuhni paskau batatanhna kat, yaka bik kaput dukih yuyulwarang kat.

Lanibin 17.

Sau wisamni yalahwa pani papus kingni sauni walanibis palni balna kidi ramhnina duduwa ki, Nikarawah walanibis kalnin yulna atnin, dawak witinna saunina kaupak dakwi kalahwas ban yulna atnin sip ki, dawak sip karang yulna atnin gabamint parasni duwa anipis as kau, muihni kidi Nikarawah sauni pas akat yalahwa kat.

Lanibin 18.

Gabamint lani yamwa anipis rikni kidi sip karang ki, Nikarawah walanibis kapat yulwi yakna atnin, king uk sauna walanibis as, Nikarawah yulwi wark nuhni minitbayak yamna muihni as kau.

Lanibin 19.

King sauni uk walanibis kalna kidi sip karang Nikarawah walanibis kalna atnin, tanit kau kingni sauni kaupak dakwi kalahna atnin ki, dawak bik, parasni duwa anipis as kau yulna karang ki, angdika mani, adika lani kau ais yulwa bitik kdikika barangni yamwi duwa karang kat.

Lanibin 20.

Nikarawah walanibis as kau bik, Nikarawah walanibis kapat wauhnitaya duwa kidi sip dakyakwas karang. King sauni uk walanibis kalna yulni, Nikarawah walanibis wauhnitaya duwa kidi diswas karang.

Lanibin 21.

Nikarawah sauni walanibis kalnin, disnin dawi watwi dunin tani balna kalaih la balna munah barangni yamna karang.

Lanibin 22.

King sauni bu walanibis atnin kalaih, kingni sauni bu biri biri ampat kalpakwi lani yayamna munah lani barangni yamna karang.

YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI IV

NIKARAWAHMUIHNI BALNA RAMHNINA, YAMNIN

DUWA, DAWI TANIT DAKWA BALNA

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA I

MUIH BANI RAMHNINA

Lanibin 23.

Muih sangnika ramhni kidi, sip minit bayakwas ki, dawak muih kapat dini palni ki. Nikarawah akat la munah muih inin lani dis ki.

Lanibin 24.

Muih alas kidi di yamnin duwa ki, wanaihni yulni, pani yulni, king sauni yulni, dawi muih bitik yulni bik. Muih bani ramhni kidi kidi witwa ki, muih uk ramhni duwa balna yakarak, muih bitik sangnika tanit daknin daknin yulni dawi muih bitik yamnini yuyulwa yulni kat.

Lanibin 25.

Muih alas kidi ramhni duwa ki:

1) Muihni kat pri ni lani.

2) Sangnika tanit daknin.

3) Muihni nuhni dawi la munah parasni duwa kidi rsinipik kalana

atnin.

Lanibin 26.

Muih slas kidi ramhni duwa ki:

1) Alas sangnika lani dunin dawi unipas sangnika dunin bik.

2) Unipas kau muih kawi lani bahnin awas, muih uk wauhtaya awas kat di sisihwa kat muih uk yaklauwi laihwi talnin lani dis ki.

3) Muihni kalkulna lani dawi ayangni yamnini risnipik kalana atnin.

4) Gabamint muihni balna witin yulni ais duduwa balna kidi amanglanin lanin ramhni duwa ki, dawak ais yulni dawi ais yamnin yulni kidika yulni duduwa pa amanglanin ramhni duwa ki.

Muih as uni pas kau kiwi kanin sip karang ki, La wihta parasni duwa yaklauwi anidar kalawa munah,

Kaunah sip karang kana atnin:

a) upas kau kawa muihni balna yaklauwi yaka unipas yak la nining kauh dini balna yamwa bang ki atwarang kat, awaskat pas yakaupak ilp yuyulwarang kat;

b) kuh buswa karang kat, awas kat kakahni dutni as yulni upas muihni balna sangninaka disninna sip kat, awaskat uni disnin sip karang kat;

c) upas yak talwas muihni uiwi kakana kat, dawak muihni balna kidi di dutni yamnin trai talwa bang karang kat;

d) la bahna muihni as laihnin atwi uywi kiwa awarni balna yak kiri kiwi yakat kaki yakmalda sak karang kat;

e) muih malwi duwi kiuna muihni as laih yaknin yulni kat. Adika dini bitik pas akat la munah yamna karang ki.

La yaklauwi laih yulwa ki, wauhtaya satni balna, numba kulwi yakna, dawi wauhtaya sat uk uk bik, laihwi talnin yulni kat, kidika bitik, la wihta balna awaskat, di minit daknin wihnita balna laihwi talnin nitni sak karang kat.

Wauhtaya ulwi sihna, dawak muih wauhnitaya sat uk uk balna, la pirini kaupak laihwi yayakna balna bitik kidi la yamwa paskau, awaskkat ahni yak bik parasni dis karang ki.

Lanibin 27.

Muih bitik kidi la muhni yak papatni ki, dawak papatni tanit daknin ramhni duwa ki. Di awas kapat klakulnin lanin lani dis karang ki, batanhna pani yulni, King sauni as paskaupak yulni, king tingnita paskau kanin walwa yulni, sulani, al awaskat yal yuulni, tuni yulwa yulni suts duwa yulni, kulnin lani, sara sulani duwa yulni, lalah dis awaskat minikunni yulni.

King sauni uk kaupak muihni balna kidi Nikarawah walanibis balna karak di yamnin duduwa kidi dawak ramhnina papatni duduwa ki, Kaunah king tingnita kau kanin ramhni dawi la yulwas balna kalaih ramhnina dis ki; king sauni pas kau tingnita yulni pasyak kanin anidar dis ki.

Gabamint sauni parasni kidi rispik mundi tanitdakwa ki, adika lani rikni kau ais yulwa bitik kidi, sauni pas akat ai kana muihni bitik kau ramhni kalawa ki, dawak witin parasni dinityak tanitdakna karang.

Lanibin 28.

Nikarawaha walanibis balna, king sauni uk bang kidika bik gabamint sauni parasni yaklauwi tanit dakwi mint talwa ki, kidika bitik laih yamwa karang ki, gabamint tingta yamnin tada king sauni uk sihna muihni balna munah.

Lanibin 29.

Muih bitik kidi ramhni duwa ki pri lani kat yul kulnin, sinsni lani dunin, dawi suts lani as laihnin awaskat laihwas atnin. Muih as kau bik taihna lani karak diyamwi adika ramhni dakyakwas karang, dawak taihna lani karak diyamwak sutsni yulni, kulnin lani, ais kau kulwa balna kidi yul yakwas karang.

Lanibin 30.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi ramhni duwa ki, yul kulwa balna kidi pri yulwi yaknin, muih mahni awaskat, bulah, aslah aslah karak awaskat dakni bin as kau yulwi yakna atnin, yulbauwa munah, ulva munah awaskat tani uk munah bik.

Lanibin 31.

Nikarawah walanibis balna ramhnina duduwa ki, king sauni aiska pas akat awaihna atnin, dawak kin sauni pas akat, angdika pani yak karang bik sip ki pani duwi yalahnin; king sauna pas akat pri lani kat kai kanin dawak kalahnin ramhni duwa ki.

Lanibin 32.

Muih as kau bik taihna lani karak diyamwak yamwas karang ki, La kau yulwas dini balna kidi, dawak bik taihna lani karak kalawak laihwatwas karang, La yaklauwi putwas dini balna kidi.

Lanibin 33.

Muih as bik, ban kaupak muih aslah kulnin lani yulni, silak uni awaskat prisin uni kau awas karang ki, dawak prini lani bik dakyakwas karang ki, La munah ais yulwa yakat dawak ampat yamnin tani ukna balna munah sip karang ki, yamna atnin. Kaput bahang:

1) Silak kau laih watnin sip karang ki, La wihta parasni duwa muihni as adar ulwi sihwa karak manah, awaskat La yaklauwi parasni kalana muihni balna ulwi sihwarang kat, Kaunah sip karang yamna atnin awarni yak ramh palni laih la bahwa karang kat.

2) Silak kau laihwatna muihni bani kidi ramhni duwa ki:

2.1 Laih watwi duwas isningpaknin karang ki, witin amanglawa tuni kau, dawak yayahni kaupak kalyulna karang, ais yulni silak kau laihwatwa tannika, dawak witin niningkauh ais yulni dutni yayakwa kidika; tingparah balna dawak witin palni yaklauwi bik kalawak wanaihni balna kau isning paknin ki; dawak bik muih mayang lani kat, risnipik karak di yamna karang ki.

2.2 Silak kau laihwatna kaupak awar 48 bayakwa pasyakat pri yaknin karang ki, awaskat, La yamnin parasni duwa tingni kau lanin karang ki.

3) La bahna minit lauwi bayakna kaupak, la parasni duwa muihni yaklauwi silak kaupak yaknin anidar lana kaupak, muih as bik baisa silak uni kau sak atnin anidar dis ki.

4) La minit bayakwi muih kau silak awa bani kidi, La parasni duwa muihni niningyak kakahni kaiwa karang, warkni yulni dawak la bahna yulni bik.

5) Muih silak uni kau duwa tanituna balna kidi, trai tatalna karang, silak muihni baisa lani yamwa balna, dawak lani yamwi paat kau rumhna balna kidi wayah yakwi pani uk uk kau duduwa atnin.

Lanibin 34.

Lani yamwa muihni bani kidi ramhni duwa ki, papatni lani kat, adika sangnika tanit dakna balna bikikisni balna adika:

1) Kakahni dis muihni kapat kalkulna karang, La yamwa munah patni duwa kidi baisa nining kawas karang mani.

2) Laihwatwas palni Lani yamna karang ki, la yaklauwi, La wihta parasni kalana muihni balna yaklauwi. Silak muihni kidi la wihta uk tingni kau murwi lanin lani diski. Muih as bik La wihta lani yamwa tingni kaupak yakwi, la wihta uk tingni kau duwi lanin yamnin lani dis ki.

3) La yaklauwi kapat yamnin pakna balna kidi, La amanglayang dakni as tingni kau lanin karang ki, lani kidi yamnin yulni. Lani yamna kidi watwi laihwi talnin lani kidi awi dawa ki.

4) Lani yamwa tuna kaupak sansni kalana karang, witin dawi tanitdakwa muihni kidi yulbaunin, kaput bik sansni kalana karang, ma ampus dawak ampat tanitdaknin tani duwa atnin yulni.

5) La yamwa tuna bahwa kaupak tanitdakyang muihni dis karang kat, gabamint yaklauwi layar as pakna karang, tanitdaknin yulni; awaskat la awa wauhnitaya ulwi sihna mani kaupak tanitdaknin muihni pakwas karang kat.

La kau duwa muihni kidi ramhni duwa ki, tanitdakwa laniyar karak pri lani kat albulah yulbauwa atnin.

6) La muhni kau sak awa mani ramhni duwa ki, tuni wirlayang muihni as duna atnin, dawak, La wihta ais tuni kau yuyulwa kat wirlawi tanitdakwa atnin.

7) Taihna lani karak kalyamwak alas niningkauh yulwas karang ki, maritni awaskat yalni awaskat alni niningkau yulwas karang ki, kaput bik wanaihni balna ani palni muihni balna dawak maritni wanaihni balna niningkauh bik, kaput bik taihna lani karak yamwak trabil duyang kapat yulwi yakwas karang ki.

8) La yaklauwi ma ampus pas yak lan yamwi saran laihni yulna yakat, lani yamwi saran laihnin karang ki, la yamnin anipis parasni bani yak.

9) La bahna yulni, lani yamwi pat kau rumhna mani, ramhni duwa ki, wihta baisa parasni duwa muhni kau kiwi lani walna atnin, lani yamna kidi watwi laihwi talnin yulni.

10) La as bahna yulni la yamwi bayakwi silak kau awah kawi lauwi bayakna awaskat, kawi bayakwi pri ni lani duna kat, sim patni yulni watwi la kau duwi kiwas karang ki.

11) La tingni kau duwi uywas karang, dawak la awi pat kau bik rumwas karang, trabil as yamna awaskat di as yamwas dana yulni, kidika mani balna yak, dini kidika, La yaklauwi La bahna dini kapat kalkulwas dai kat, kaput bik La ukna paskau yulwas dini as yulni sip muih as kau pat tingni kau awas karang ki. Adar diski La as dini balna kat ulwi yaknin awas kat, silak muihni kau tanka dutni karak kalyamna atnin.

Silak anin yulni la yamna kidi param laih yamwarang ki. Wingkau ulwi yakwa balna dawak muih bitik muhni yak laih apis tanitdakna karang ki, muih kulnin risnipik dawi pa muihni la kat yalahwa risnipik yulni.

Pat kalyamna muihni kidi La yamwa tuna kaupak, dawak La yamwa parasni pisni bani yak, lani yamwa pasyak awi duna karang ki.

Lanibin 35.

Muih baisasa balna kidi, la satni as pas kau anin anidar dis karang ki, kaput bik, La wihta la yamwa satni as kau bik anin anidar diski. Muih baisasa balna la bahna muihni balna kidi sip silak satni pani as kau duwi kiwas karang, dawak parasni duwa anipis as apatnah amanglayang balna tingnina kau dana karang.

Adika yulni laih, La as yamna karang adika dini yulni kat.

Lanibin 36.

Muih bitik kidi ramhni duwa ki, muihni nuhni, sinsni lani dawak muhni risnipik kalana atnin. Muih as bik wisnin lani kau awas karang, dutni waraunin, dawak muih awas kapat kalyamnin lani kau awas karang ki. Adika ramhni minit bayakwi yamwarang kat, la bahna as kalkulna karang dawak, la tingni kau bik bukna karang.

Lanibin 37

. Kakahnil as yulni la yaklauwi pat kau rumhwa muihni kidika, muihni minit yakat manah bukwa ki. La bahna yulni pat kau rumhwa kidika, patni aslah, awaskat, uk uk libitwi kulwi yakwak, pat tingni kau kurih 30 minitkau bayakwas karang ki.

Lanibin 38.

Bayakna mani kau la parasni disna as karak sip watwi yakarak yus munwi la yamwas karang, silak anin yulni la yamwa yakat manah sip karang, kaunah, silak muihni ilpni kau karang kat.

Lanibin 39.

Nikarawah akat, Silak Uni tunan wawauhwa kidika muih muihni nuhni risnipik kalawi warkni yayamwa ki, dawak yaka munah ramh palnin yamnin wawalwa kidika kuduh, silak kau bukna muihni kidika tani tani sumalwi dulauwi watwi pa muihni sangnika paskau ana atnin. Tani tani sumalwi dulauwi yakarak, diparaswi kiwa ki, silak muihni kidika wanaihni karak watwi aslah kalahnin, muihni sangnika yamni dunin, skul dunin, yamni yalahnin, dawak silak uni paskau wark yamwa kidika minit bik kalwa atnin ki. Silak kau bukna yakaupak watwi di sumalwi dulaunin walwa ki.

Yayal silak kau bubukwa kidika, ahal balna sinilak uni karak wayah yakwi pa uk kau duduwa karang, dawak bik, silak main talyang idika bik, yayal balna karang.

Lanibin 40.

Muih as bik albah lani kau bukwas karang. Albah lani dawi muih laihwi pa ukkau dulawi bakannin lani satni bitik kidi la yaklauwi adar kalawas ki.

Lanibin 41.

Muih as bik taras yulni silak bukwas karang. Kulnin lani tuhni adi karak, laih watwas karang ki, muih as walanibis kau plun minit kalawas kat, la wihta parasni duwa as yaklauwi, la parsani minityak ilna atnin. Nikarawah walanibis, awaskat King sauni uk walanibis bik salahni yak duwa ki, ais taras duwa kat launa atnin.

Lanibin 42.

Nikarawah akat, sau uk muihni as, sauni kau trabil duwa yulni dawak kingni yaklauwi usnit uywa kat, ramhni duwa ki kaiwak tanit dakna atnin. Kapahni yulni dawak kingni usnit uywak tanit daknin kidika laih, yamna karang, muihni kidika tawan yulni, pri lani yulni, la yulni dawak muih ramhni minit kalbauwa yulni kingni yaklauwi usnit uhuywa kat.

La yaklauwi muihni yak ampat tanit daknin tani balna ana karang ki, yaka bik Nikarawah king sauni uk balna karak kalpakwi yul bauwi la rawawasna balna munah. Ma sauki pas akat, tanitdakwa muihni as, sauni akaupak kangwi yaknin nitni sak karang kat, muihni yak usnit uywa kingni sauni kau laih nawatwi sihwas karang ki.

Lanibin 43.

Nikarawah akaupak, pulitik awaskat, la bahna as king tingta yamwa kau kanin walwa karak libitna kat, sauni akaupak walanibis as King sauni uk kau silak anin yulni sihwas karang ki, Nikarawah lani kulwa yakat. La bahna as yulni King sauni uk kau silak kau sihnin kidi, King sauni mahni kalpakwi la yayamna munah yamna karang.

Nicaragua walanibis as bik sauni akaupak King sauni uk kau silak anin sihwas karang ki.

Lanibin 44.

Muih di duwa balna, pa as kaupak dulanin sip dini, dawak dulanin sip awas U, sau, tingta yamnin dini balna kapat kidka daniwan yak risnipik kalawa ki.

Yulwa dini balna kidika pa muihni baraknin kau paraswa bahang, ramhni kalawa adika, tingta yamwi kiwa yakat, pa muihni bitik yamnini yulni nitnina pasyak kawa ki. Pa uk kau dulanin sip awas dini balna kidi La munah tingni dakyaknin sip karang ki, kaunah tanit kaupak minit yamni as launa karang.

Gabamint di dahnin warkni yamnin yulni, sau pisni nuhni warkni yayamwas dauh bang balna kidi dakyaknin yulni kat, La yaklauwa ulwi yakna karang, ampat yamnin, nuhnini, ampat launin dawi mint pakwi launin tannika bitik, sauni pisni lauwi bayaknin ahana yakat.

Muih dini yamnin dakyamknin anidar diski. Gabamint tingta yamwai muihni as adika lani minit bayakwarang kat, witin ais dini yamni duwa karak minit launa karang ki, ais dini dutni yamna bitik yulni.

Lanibin 45.

Angdika muihni as talwak lani rikni akat yulwa balna kau ramhni duwa kidi mint bayakwa kat, awas kat, mint bayaknin awa kat, sip karang ki, la walnin Gabamint yaklauwi muihni nuhni tanitdaknin lani, muihni nunhni awaskat, lani rikni kau ramhni balna tanitdaknin, lani kidi sip ki walna atnin, La yaklauwi ampat yamnin tani ukwi dana yaka munah.

Lanibin 46.

King sauni pas akat muih bitik kidika gabamint parasni yaklauwi tanitdakwa ki, dawak bik, muih kapat, muih bitik muihni nuhni risnipik duduwa yakat risnipik kalawa ki, muih ramhnina duwa kidi, laihwatwas risnipik kalawi, paraswi dawak tanitdakwa ki, dawak parasni kalawa ki, Adika lani nunuhni Sau Bitik Muihni Ramhnina; Muih Al Ramhni dawak Yamnin Duwa Yulni Sau Wisamni Yalahwa Pni kau yayamna kau yulwa kidi; Mukuntak baraknin, Muih sangnika Yalahwa bitik Ramhnina Yulni Sau Bitik La Ulwi yayakna kidi, dawak Sau Wisamni Yalahwa Pani Ramhnina Yulni Kaluduhwi Yayakna kau ais yulwa bitik kidi yamna atnin.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA II

KING TINGTA YAMWA PASYAK KANIN

YAK RAMHNI DUWA KIDI

Lanibin 47.

Pa dawan kalkulna ki, Nikarawah walanibis balna kurih salap minitkau tiaskau kuihna muihni balna kidi.

Pa dawan balna manah, ramhni duwa ki, adika Lani Rikni dawak la uk balna kau pulitik yulwa kidika laihwi yus munnin, kidi laih kurihni uywi kuihna atnin yulwa kidika manah ki.

Pa dawan ramhnina kidika laih watwa ki, muih trabil dutni as yamwi silak bukna kat, as, awaskat, trabil as yamna yulni la yamni bayakwi muihni yak di as as yamnin sip awas kapat la yaklauwi pakna kat.

Lanibin 48.

La awi dawa ki, Nikarawah walanibis bitik kidika ramhnina papatni duwa ki, king tingta yamwa paskau kawi warkni yamna atnin, dawak ais yamnin salahni kau duwa balna bitik kidi yakat yamna atnin bik, ahal dawak yayal karak papatni palni di kulna ki.

Gabamint sauni parasni salahni yak sakki Nikarawah walanibis balna sangninaka duduwa pasyak, papatni aws lani balna bang kat, laihwi dispamnin, dawi sansni kalawak king sauni pas akat, pulitik warkni, lalahn baraknin dawi pa muihni sangnika warkni yaman atnin.

Lanibin 49.

Nikarawah akat kaluduhna atnin ramhni duduwa ki, tawan nuhni dawi asangpas warkni kalahyang balna, yayal balna, wawahma sirarau, di dahwi sahyang balna, tingkau di yamyang balna, skul saran laihna balna, sins muihni balna, aiwanyang satni balna, suts muihni balna, Atlantik kus muihni balna dawi pa muihni bitik, ahni kau danin lani dis karak, ais dukih diawa kidi parasdak uywi kuihnin yulni manah, kaput munah, pa muihni sangnika wisamni as bina atnin yulni.

Adika kaluduhna balna kidi yayamna karang pa dawan balna kulninna lani kat kaluduhwi, tatuna walwi yakwa munah, pa baraknin warkni yayamwa karang, pulitik daknini, awaskat, daknini awas atnon bik sip ki, ais tingta yayamnin kukulwa yakat.

Lanibin 50.

Pa muihni balna kid ramhni duduwa ki papatni king sauni tingnita pasyak kawi tingta yayamna atnin. La as yayamna karang, king sauna aiska dawi, sauna pisni as yak bik, yakarak tani duwi yamna karang.

Lanibin 51.

Pa dawan balna ramhnina duduwa ki, tuna walwi yaknin yayamwa mani, tatuna walwi yaknin dawak witin yak tatuna kapat walwi papakna atnin, dawak gabamint warkni bik laihna atnin, kaunah sip ki kal laihwatna atnin, adika Lani Rikni, laihwatnin dini pakna balna yulni.

Pa dawan balna yamnin duwa ki, dakni kau kalpakwi la yamnin papaiwarang kat, La yaklauwi sansni kalawarang manah sip karang yamwas atnin.

Lanibin 52.

Pa dawan balna ramnhni duduwa ki, Gabamnit Tingni Rikni Parasni balnan kau di yulna atnin, di dudutni yayamwa kat yulwi yakna atnin watwi barangni yamnin yulni kat, alas alas awaskat dakni kau kalahwi yamna atni sipki; dawak biri bik sirihni nangnit lana karang, kaput bik, sirihni isning pakna karang, ais as isningpaknin kat, ampat la kau yulwa yakat.

Lanibin 53.

Sanni lani kat kaluduhwi yulbaunin kidi rispik munwa ki; adika ramhni tingta yamnin laih tanit kaupak adar yaknin nit awas ki.

Lanibin 54.

Muih mahni kaluduhnin, kaluduhwi lapaknin, tapas kau kalahwi lapaknin ramhni kidika rispik munwa ki, bitik kidi La kau ais yulwa yakat.

Lanibin 55.

Nikarawah upa dawan balna king tingta yamwa pasyak kanin walwa dakni paskau kanin ramhni duduwa ki, King parasni usnit uiwi laihnin tani walna atnin bik.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA III

PA MUIHNI BARAKNIN RAMHNINA

Lanibin 56.

Gabamnit sauni parasni yaklauwi, baraknin tingta bitik paskau muih sakahwa kalna balna kau diparasna karang, dawak war kau bubukna wanaihni balna bitik kau parasna karang.

Lanibin 57.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi, muih kapat wark kalahnin ramhnina duduwa ki.

Lanibin 58.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi skul kanin dawi sinsni baraknin ramhni duduwa ki.

Labinin 59.

Nikarawah walanibis balna muihni nuhni yamni dunin papatni ramhni duduwa ki. Gabamint yaklauwi warkni tani yamwarang, diparasnin, tanit daknin, dawi watwi sangnika dulaunin yulni.

Gabamnit parasni tingni yak sakki, muih sangnika yamni dunin warkni tunan duwi yamna atnin, dawak pa muihni balna kau diparasna karang warkni tanit daknin yulni.

Pa dawan balna sakahni yak sakki, pa ampat klin dunin lani ahawarang kidi laihwi yamna atnin.

Lanibin 60.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi ramhnina duduwa ki, pa sangnika yamni as kau yalahnin. Gabamint parasni salahni yak sakki sau sangnika dawi sau ritsni balna mint talnin, tanit daknin dawak watwi dulauna atnin.

Lanibin 61.

Gabamnit yaklauwi Nikarawah walanibis balna kau, Sangnika Yamnin Tanitdaknin ramhni kidi kalawa ki, ma sangkika dawak wark malahwa paskyak ais mai kalahwarang yulni, ampat munah yamnin kidika la ais yulwa yakat yamna karang.

Lanibin 62.

Muih sakahwa kakalna balna yulni Gabamint yaklauwi di parasnin tingta satni dulauna karang, watwi muihni nuhni, sinsni lani dawak skul duna balna dulauna atnin, kaput bik tingta paskau ana karang.

Lanibin 63.

Nikarawah walanibis ramhnina duduwa ki, yuh niningkauh tanit dakna atnin. Gabamint yaklauwi baraknin tingta dulauna karang di kasnin yamni duwa atnin yulni, dawak papatni sakyakwi diawa lani balna bik.

Lanibin 64.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi ramhni duwa ki, U yamni, alahni dawak upas muihni balna barangni tanitdakwa kat kakawa atnin. Gabamint parasni yaklauwi wark yamna karang adika ramhni uywi kuihnin yulni.

Lanibin 65.

Nikarawah walanibis balna ramhni duduwa ki, yakisdinin, muihni iksasais dunin, alasna dunin dawak muih uk karak kalpaknin. Gabamint yaklauwi parasna karang yakisdinin dawak iksasais satni skulni duna atnin, pa muihni balna mahni uduhwi duwi sumalwa munah, Nicaragua walanibis balna muihni

aiska baraknin yulni kat. Adika warkni laih baraknin warkni yayamni karak yamna karang.

Lanibin 66.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi, di kalahwa yulni ramh palni kidi dakanin ramhni duwa ki. Adika ramhni pasyak kawa ki, pri lani karak walnin, dunin dawak yulni dawi kulnin lani yulwi yaknin, yul bauwa karak manah, awaskat, ulwa munah, di waya yamwa munah, awaskat, tani uk as angdika baisa yus munnin kulwarang kat.

Lanibin 67.

Di kalahwa balna yulwi yaknin ramhni kidi pa muihni kau risnipik kalanin bik salahni yak sakki, dawak warkni adika yamwa ki, Gabamnit Lani Rikni kau ais yulwa risnipik yakat. Adiak ramhni duwa kidi putnin sip awas karang ki, ma takit kau ais tranibil kaiwarang yulni La munah tanit dakwa yakat.

Lanibin 68.

Di kalahwa balna yulwi yakyang kamnipani balna kidi, pa muihni kau di parasnin tingta yamwa pasyakat, King sauna aiska baraknin warkni yak bik parsnin karang ki.

Nikarawah walanibis balna kidi, di kalahwa balna yulwi yakyang kamnipani kawi yus munnin sansni duwa atnin ki, dawak ramhnina dawi tanitdakwa balna kau trabil di yamna kat, laih yakwi kalyulnin ramhni duduwa ki.

Gabamint parasni kidi mint talna karang, di kalahwa balna yulwi yakyang kamnipani balna kidi, King uk sauni kaupak muihni balna tingnina yak buknin awas yulni, awaskat, dakni as kalahwi puwi duna atnin awas yulni. La yaklauwi adika tingta yamna karang.

Di kalahwak yulwi yakyang kamnipani balna, ulna kau, wing munah, wayani munah yayakwa balna, wauhtaya, misin dawak tingta kau yus munwa dini balna satni sau uk kaupak bakanwi duwi aiwa balna kidi, kaput bik, sau uk kaupak wauhtaya bakanwi duwi aiwi wauhi bakakanwa balna, wauhtaya bikis, di waya, skul wauhnitaya, sumalnin dini, ma bani ulwi yakna balna, bakanwa yulni, alkaldi, Alas yalahwa gabamnitni, dawak gabamintni rikni yaklauwi tunan minit dakwas karang.

Tunan mint daknin lani balna yaklauwi tunan duna karang. Di kalahwa balna yulwi yakyang kamnipani, gabamnit dini, awas kat dakni uk dini balna karang bik, tanit kaupak sip laih watwas karang ki. Mani as bik, trabil as kalahna wayani kapat yus munnin yulni, tingni dakyakwas karang, muih lulnin lani wauhtaya ulwi yakwa misinni as bik dakyakwas karang.

Lanibin 69.

Muih bitik kidika, alas alas awaskat dakni kau kalahwi, angdika sutsni kau kulwa yamwa atnin ramhni duwa ki, muh bulah awskat muih mahni muhni kau, prias yamwa munah, tingta yamwi awaskat sumalwa munah.

Muih as bik La balna kidi yamwas dawi bayakwas karang ki, kaput bik, muih ramhni duwa balna dawi yamnin duwa balna kidi sip laih watwas karang ki, muih as kulnin lani awaskat, suts as kulnin lani karak.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA IV

UPAS MUIHNI RAMHNINA

Lanibin 70.

Upas muihni kidika kuduh pa muihni isning palni ki, dawak pa muihni dawi gabamint parasni yaklauwi tanitdaknin ramhni duwa ki.

Lanibin 71.

Nikarawah walanibis ramhnina ki, upas muihni as duna atnin. Upas muihni dini yamni balna kidi tanit dakwa ki, dawi la awi tingni dakyakwas karang dawak gabamint yaklauwi tunan mint dakwas karang. Ramhni balna adika, La yaklauwi tunan duwarang dawak tanitdakna karang.

Muihbinina kidi barangni tanitdakna karang, dawak muihbin kapat ramhni satni bitik kalana karang, kidika yulni kat, King Sauni Mahni Kalpakwi Muih Binina Lani Yayamna kidika parasni aiska duwa ki.

Lanibin 72.

Marit lani dawak muih yalni kapat duwi yalahwa kidika Gabamint parasni yaklauwi tanitdakwa ki; kidika laih, al dawak yal karak yulbauwi wilin kalahwi kulnin aslah barangni kalalahna ki, dawak sip karak dakwi kalahnin witinna kulninna lani kat, awaskat as yaklauwi kapat yulwarang kat. La yaklauwi adika dini laihwi yamna karang.

Lanibin 73.

Upas muihni karak kalpakwi yalahnin kidika, rispik lani, biri biri kalparasnin lani dawak papatni lani sak atnin ki, al dawak yal karak ramhnina dawak ais yamnin duwa balna bik pasyakat.

Di almuk alna kidi upas nitnina bitik barangni yamwa atnin ki, dawak walaninabis, al bu kalparaswi skul awi muhninna ki, al bupak ramhnina dawak yamnin dini duwa balna kidi papatni salahnina yak sak ki. Walanibis balna bik, salahnina yak sakki, dini almuk balna kau, risnipik kalanin dawi diparasnin. Yamnin dini dawak ramhnina yulwa laih, adika yulni La yayamna munah laihwi yamna karang.

Lanibin 74.

Gabamint parasni yaklauwi muih kalsahnin mahnin lani kidika tanit dakwa ki.

Yal muihkaruk kalna mani yamni tanitdakna lani as duwarang dawak wark paskaupak ris lani kalana karang, minit duwa yakarak, kaput bik, muihni sangnika tanit daknin yulni bik minit kalana karang ki.

Muih as bik yal as muihkaruk yulni tingta yamnin kalawas atnin sip awas karang, dawak muhkaruk kalna mani awaskat, muihbin duwi bayakna mani sip tingta pani kaupak kangwas karang ki; bitik kidika La munah laihwi yamna karang.

Lanibin 75.

Muih walanibis bitik kidi ramhnina papatni duduwa ki. Wanaihni ayangni pakwa yak, ahni pirini kau danin lani dis karang ki. La bitik pasyakat, walanibis as as kau baisa nuhni awaskat papatni awas lani balna yayamwa karang kat, as bik parasni dis karang ki.

Lanibin 76.

Gabamint parsani yaklauwi muih baisasa balna tanitdaknin yulni baraknin warkni lani satni dulauna karang ki; adiak pasyakat ramhnina duduwa karang ki, tanitdaknin, minittalnin dawak skul kalana atnin ais nitni yakat, wanaihni balna, Pa muihni dawak gabamint parasni yaklauwi.

Lanibin 77.

Muihbarak balna kidika ramhnina duduwa ki, tanitdakna atnin, wanaihni, Pa muihni dawak Gabamint parasni yaklauwi.

Lanibin 78.

Gabamint parasni yaklauwi papang dawak itang lani yamni yayamwa kidika tanitdakwa ki. Papangi awaskat itangni pa tannika walwi talnin ramhni bik la ni ulwi dawa ki.

Lanibin 79.

Walabis as walanibis kapat duwi muhnin ramhni kidika la kau ulwi dawa ki, muihbin kidika muihni nuhni aiska yamni baraknin palni yulni. Lani yaklauwi laihwi barangni yamna karang.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA V

TINGTA KALAHNIN RAMHNINA

Lanibin 80.

Tingta kalahnin kidi ramhni as ki, dawak pa muihni baraknin salahni yak sak ki. Nikarawah walanibis balna wark kalahnin kidika munah kuduh, pani muihni dawak muih balna nitnina barangni yayamwa kidika, dawak King sauni ritsni dawi baraknin lani dukauwa ki. Gabamint yaklauwi trai talwarang, Nikarawah walanibis balna bitik kau diawak tingta yamwi minik yakwa karang, dawak muih kapat ramhnina diawak wark yayamwa karang.

Lanibin 81.

Tingta muihni balna kidi ramhnina duwa ki, kampani tunan dunin pasyak kawi wark yamna atnin, witinna kaluduhna duduwa munah dawak La ais yulwa yakat.

Lanibin 82.

Tingta muihni balna kidi ramhnina duwa ki wark as kalahwa kat adika yamnini balna duwa atnin:

1.- Minit papat duna atnin, sim warkni yamna yulni, dawak tingta pani yamnin bik papatni duna atnin, pa muihni paskau ais tingta yamwa lani yakat, pirini kau danin lani dis karang ki, King tingta yamwa pasyak kanin walwa yulni, sutsni yulni, pa muihni as yulni, al awaskat yal kidika yulni, awaskat, di uk yulni karang bik, dawak tingta kalawak sangnika yami duwi muih kapat yalahna karang.

2.- Minit launa karang ki, parasni duwa lalahni karak dawak, tingta yamwa pani kat.

3.- Tingta yamwa minit dibin palni dawak sangnika tanitdaknin dakwa balna kidi sip laih watwas karang ki, kaunah unipas muihni balna tanidaknin yulni laihtak, dawak bik la munah yamna karang ki.

4.- Tingta pani yamni as dunin karang ki, muihni nuhni, muihni sangnika, muihni klinni dawak warkni yamwa kaupak sangnika kau trabil kalyamnin niningkau tanitdaknin lani balna duwa atnin ki.

5.- Yingta mani as awar tiaskau bas duwa karang ki, sandi bani ris dunin, bakisan mani, King sauni aiska kau tingta dis mani kidi minit lauwi kalanin karang ki, kaput bik, waiku salap minit kau bas minit bik kalanin karang ki, bitik kidika La ais yulwa yakat.

6.- Tingta pani yak nining wark duwa atnin sansni kalanin, tingta baisa yamni kau ilnin sansni papatni duna atnin ki, yaka pasyak di uk talwas karang ki, ma ampus tingta kalahna kidi, warkni yamna, wark yamni yamwa tannika dawak kasuk palni yamwa tannika balna manah.

7.- Sangnika aiska tanitdaknin ramhni dawak kaswi sangka yalahnin dini balna bik kalanin, di sip awas kalahwarang, muihbarak kalwarang mani, tingta kaupak sakahwa kalahwarang mani, siknis kalwarang awaskat, muihbin dunin mani balna yak ilp duini yulni; kaput bik, wanaihni as dauwarang mani yus munnin yulni, bitik kidika ampat dawak ais yamnin kidika La munah yamna karang ki.

Lanibin 83.

Tingta laihwatnin ramhni kidika kalawa ki.

Lanibin 84.

Muih baisasa balna tingta kalahnin anidar diski, muihni yamni baraknin kau awaskat, skulni kawi baraknin kau kakahni kalyamnin tingta balna kapat kau. Muih binina dawak Apis bararak balna kau tanitdakna karang wark awi waraunin, awaskat, minit launin kau waraunin awas yulni.

Lanibin 85.

Tingta muihni balna kidika, skul dunin, sinsni lani baraknin, tingta tannika lan kalnin ramhni duwa ki; Gabamint yaklauwi tingta tani satni karak parasna karang.

Lanibin 86.

Nikarawah walanibis bitik kidi ais skulni dunin dawak ais tingta kalahnin kidi walwi dunin ramhni duwa ki

,

dawak tingta pani as walwi duna atnin

,

yaka pasyak skulni saran laihna wauhnitaya manah nit karang, dawak pa baraknin dini as yamwa atnin.

Lanibin 87.

Nikarawah pas akat tingta muihni kaluduhnin ramhni param duwa ki. Tingta muihni balna kidika alas kulnin lani kat, tingta kaluduhna balna yamnin sip karang ki, dawak adika bik, La kau ais yulwa yakat yayamna karang ki. Tingta muihni as kau bik, taihwi kalyamwak, tingta muihni kaluduhna as pas kanin anidar dis ki, dawak kaluduhna as paskau sak yakaupak bik kalyamwak dawi kalahnin anidar dis ki. Tingta muihni kaluduhna balan alas lanina duwi tingta kalahnin ramhni kidi rispik munwa ki, dawak tingta muihni kaluduhna parasni kidi rispik munwa ki.

Lanibin 88.

Tingta muihni balna ramhnina laih watnin sip awas dini balna kidi tanitdakwi rispik munwa ki, yaka munah, muihni bani awaskat, daknini yamnini tanitdaknin, kidika yulni kampani daniwan karak ayangni rawawasna karang:

1) Muih alas alas kannitrak.

2) Daknini yulbauwi barangna lani. Bupak pasyak, la ais yulwa yakat yayamwarang.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA VI

ATLANTIK KUS KAU PA BININA RAMHNINA

Lanibin 89.

Atlantik kus pani binina balna kidi Nikarawah pani sahyaknin sip awas pisni as ki, kaput bahang sim ramhnina duduwi, dawak ais yamnin dini balna bik papatni duduwa ki.

Atlantik kus kau pani binina kidi ramhni duduwa ki, sara mani kaupak sulani laniduduwa kidika tanitdakwi barakna atnin, King sauni aiska aslah pas yakat; Alas ampat kaluduhnin lani duwa kidi yus munna karang dawak witinna dinana kidi tunan duna atnin, witinna sara mani kaupak ampat yalalahwa dai lani kat.

Gabamint yaklauwi Atalntik kus kau pa binina kidi ampat saunina duwi daniwan kalwa lani kidika rispik munna karang. Kaput bik, wasni dawi asangnipas sangnika yus munwi, yamnini dunin yulni bik rispik munwa ki.

Lanibin 90.

Atlantik kus kau pani binina kidi ramhnina duduwa ki, tunina yulnin, sinsnina dawak yalahwa tannika pri lani duduwa atnin. Yalahwa tannika dawak, kulninna lani balna kidi, King sauna aiska yalahwa tannika yak baisa parasni ilwa ki. Gabamint yakaluwi baraknin warkni dulauna karang, adika ramhni laihwi yamnin yulni.

Lanibin 91.

Gabamint salahni yak duwa ki, La satni balna ana atnin, yakarak kalawak Nicaragua walanibis as bik, tuni yulwa, yalahwa tannika dawi sulanini yulni di dauh kapat pirini kau lanin awas yulni.

YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI V

KING SAUNI AISKA TANITDAKNIN

LA SAHYAKNA TUNA ASLAH

Lanibin 92.

Nikarawah Suku turukyang balna kuduh, gabamint araknibus tingni parasni ki, King sauni daklana, alas gabamnitni parasni dawi sauni aiska uduhna adi tanitdaknin yulni.

Di tannika aslah aslah yulni manah laih, Nikarawah Tatuna King kidi, gabamint tingni parasni duwa karak kaluduhwi yulbauwi talwi, sip karang ki, Nikarawah Suku turukyang balna, Tingparah yak di parasna atnin, kaunah kidika bik King sauni aiska pasyakat trabil mahni yaklauwa karang kat, pat nuhni balna awaskat, sau daukalwa kakahni balna kapat kalahwa mani yak.

King sauni pas akat, King sauni uk kaupak suku turukyang ai kawi pani duwi wark yamnin anidar diski. Sip karang adar parahni kalanin kuma kuringni, wing tinima, dawak war misinni satni balna King sauni uk kaupak, ai kawi muih sangnika kau ilp munnin tingta kapat balna laihtak, kaunah kidika bik, Ma sauki kingni yakaluwa yulna karang, dawak King sauni aiska lani yamyang parasni yakaluwi anidar kalawarang laih tak sip karang.

Lanibin 93.

Nikarawah sukuni turukyang balna kidika, King sauna aiska tingni parasni ki, arakbus warkni palni duwa ki, pulitik daknini dis ki, adar kalawa kat yamwi, yulbauwi talwi lik awas ki.

Suku turukyang muihni balna kidi tawan yalahnin tannika dawi muih ramhnina yulni sumalwi ridi yamna karang ki. Suku turukyang warkni pasyakat, la bahna awaskat kakahni bikisni yamna kat, dawak suku turukyang muihni as yamna kat, suku turukyang Lani wihta yakaluwi lani yayamwarang, ais la yulwa yakat.

Suku turukyang muihni as, La as bahna awaskat kalahni bikisni as yamna kat, La wihta aihwa balna yakalauwi lani yamna karang.

Mani as bik, pa muihni as kau, suku turukyang lani wihta as yaklauwi lani yamwas karang.

Lanibin 94.

Nikarawah sukuni turukyang dawak Tingparah muihni balna kidi King tingnita dawak king tingnita pasyak kanin wawalwa daknini tingta kapatnah yamnin sip awas karang, dawak kapatnah kaluduhna as kau wark pani dunin sip awas karang. Kaput bik, muih walwi yakwa munah tingta pani as dunin walwi yayakwa kapatnah kau kanin sip awas karang, witin suku turukyang awaskat tingparah kaupak dawi kalahwas ban tingta yamwa kat, dawak bik, angdika liksan ni kau kanin kulwa kat, baisa kurih as tanit kaupak, tingta yamwa kidi dawi kalahna atnin ki.

Adika tingta muihni balna pasyakat, kaluduhna lani, tingta pisni, tingta yamwa, tingta yamwa pisni, tingta pani kaupak baisa ma kau ilnin, tingta kau muihbarak kalna lani dawak tingta yamwi baraknin tannikka bitik kidika, kidika lanina kau ais yulwa yakat yayamna karang.

Lanibin 95.

Nikarawah sukuni turukyang balna kidi, tingta yamna karang, king sauni aiska lani rikni kau ais lakna yakat, dawak yakat rispik munna karang dawi ais yulwa yakat ulni dakana karang. Tawan muihni parasni dinit kau kana karang, dawak Nikarawah Tatuna nuhni King yaklauwi tunan duna karang, Nikarawah sukuni Turukyang Tanituna Nuhni Palni kapat, awaskat gabamint anipis parasni duwa as munah.

King saumi pas akat, sukumi turukyang dakni uk as dis karang ki, dawak bik, sukuni turukyang tingta yamwa parasni satni balna bik dis karang, la yulwa yaihnit yakat.

Lanibin 96.

Taihwi diyamwak suku turuknin tingta paskau kanin Lani diski, dawak adar dis ki, taihwi diyamwi Nikarawah sukuni turukyang dawak Tingparah tingniuta pas kau kana atnni.

Suku turukyang daknini, dawak tingparah balna dawi, gabamint anipis as kau bik adar kalawas ki, pulitik yulni muih usnit uywi pakwi talnin.

Lanibin 97.

Tingparah balna kidi ma sauki aiska, pa muhni suku turukyang ki. Tingta palni kidika kuduh, tawan pasyak barangni duwa atnin, pa dawan sangnika tanitdaknin, la bahnin tanit kau laihwatnin dawak, usnit uynin, dawak di uk balna bik, ais La yulwa yakat. Tingparah witin tingnita kat yamwa ki, tingparah daknini as dis ki, yulni kat dakawa ki, yulbauwi talwi lik awas ki. Tawan muihni parasni dinit kau kana karang, dawak King Tatuna nuhnu King yaklauwi tunan duan karang, Nikarawah Suku Turukyang Tanituna Nuhni Palni kapat, awaskat gabamint anipis parasni duwa as munah.

Tingparah tingnita yamwa pasyakat, La yamwa parsani duwa yak di paraswa karang. Tingparah tingta yamwa paskau kaluduhna tani balna kidi ampat paskau di sumalwi sahyakwi warkni yamwa yakat yayamwa ki.

YULNI TUNAN AYANGNI VI

LALAH TINGNITA, DI DAHNIN TINGNITA DAWI

GABAMINT LALAHNI TINGNITA

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA I

KING SAUNI MUKUNTAK TINGNITA

Lanibin 98.

Gabamint parasni kidi lalah warkni yamwa pasyakat tingta palni duwa kalaih, King sauni kidi kalawak barakna atnin; barakdas lani dawak sara mani kaupak muih uk kau tani ada lani kidi dispamnin; pa muihni sangninaka baisa bitar yamnin dawak ritsni balna ma ani yak baisa papatni sakyakwi diana atnin.

Lanibin 99.

Gabamnit parasni kidika, tingni yak sa ki, King sauna aiska kalawak barakna atnin, dawak muih bitik yamnini tani walyang kapat, muih bani, pa muihni, dakni balna, sau pisni dakna balan bitik nitnina barangni yamnin yulni tingnita yamwa karang ki. Gabamint parasni salahni yak sak ki, di daniwan kalahwa lani kidi tanitdaknin, parasnin dawak dulauna atnin, kaput bik, lalah baraknin tani, dakni as kamnipani, dawi gabamint kamnipani balna, di dahyang kaluduhna, pa binina kaluduhna satni bitik kidi di parasna atnin, lalah dawak pa muihni baraknin kidi kalpakwi tingnita yamna atnin.

Mukuuntak baraknin warkni yamnin bitik kidika pa muihni balna tingnita palni ki. Pa muihni dakni satni balna tingnita parasni yamwa kidika risnipik kalawa ki, kidi pasyak bang ki, pahna lani kau taldarang kat, kampani nunuhni, apis nunuhni dawak bikikisni bik, kampanibikis, didahyang kaluduhna kamnipani, kaluduhna uk uk bik.

King Mukuntak Pani Rikni kuduh gabamint tingnirah parasni duwa ki, lalah tannika tunan duna atanin. Gabamint bankni balna, dawak gabamint lalahni wauhyang anipis uk balna bik, mukuntak baraknin tingta yamyang balna karang, wark kau mukuntak awi baraknin tani walwa atnin, lalaj satni kau sahyakwi lin munwa karang, baisa palni laih, di dahyang binina dawi papus muihni balna kau di parasnin yulni. Gabamint salahni kau sak ki, tingta dawas palni, tingta yamwi tanitkau barakwi kiuna atnin. Gabamint yaklauwi pri lani kalawa ki kampani dawi bank balna warknina yamnin, kaput bik lalah warkni yamyang anipis uk balna bik, dakni as dini awaskat gabamint anipis balan karang bik, bitik kidika l amunah yamna karang. King sauni uk karak di bakannin tingnita, dini tanitdaknin dawi baisa yamni tanitdaknin, gabamint dawak kampani uk dini karang bik, biitk kidi la munah yamna karang ki.

Lanibin 100.

King sauni uk kaupak kaiwi yingta yamnin lalahni awarang kidi yulni, gabamint la as ana karang ki, yaka munah, King sauni adika lalahni dawi pa muihni barakni lani kau di parasnin yulni, King sauni daklana akat uswas tingta yamna karang.

Lanibin 101.

Tngta muihni balna, dawak tingta dulauyang daknini uk balna bik, mukuuntak baraknin tingnita ridi yamnin, tingnita yamnin dawak dulaunin ramhnina duduwa ki.

Lanibin 102.

Sau ritsni balna kidi King sauni aiska dini balna ki. Sau ritsni balna tanitdakwi yus munnin, baraknin dawak ritsni balna yaknin kidi Gabamint parasni tingni yak sakki; dawak witin sip karang ritsni balna yakwi yus munnin kannitrak rawasna atnin, King sauni nitni kapat duwarang kat.

Lanibin 103.

Gabamint yaklauwi tanitdakwa ki, pri lani kat di dawan satni balna kidi bang atnin, gabamint dini balna, muih as dini, di dahyang kaluduhna dini, pa binina aslah kalahna dini balan bitik; kidi bitik uduhna yak, lalah baraknin tingta satni kau kalahwa ki, King sauni yamnini nuhni dinit yak bang ki, dawak pa muihni baraknin warkni as yamwa ki.

Lanibin 104.

Kampani balna, adika Lani Rikni kau, di dawan kalnin satni pakwa as kau aslah kalahwi wark yamwa kidi, La muhni kau, dawi gabamint lalahni tingta yamwa muhni yak papatni kalkulna ki. Lalah baraknin tani walnin kidika pri lani duwa ki. Lalah baraknin tingta yamnin kidika barangni dawak yamwa karang, laihwatna lani as bik dis karang, pa muihni dawak King sauni aiska yamnini yulni. La ahana karang kidika manah.

Lanibin 105.

Gabamint parasni salahni yak sak ki, pa muihni yus munwa dini balna, pawa yaringni, wayalis yulnin, was, truk, truk kira tani, plin bukwa pani minit yus munnin balna kidi dulaunin, diparasnin dawak launin praisni ana atnin, kaput, amuihni balna yus munnin yak laih watwas karang ki. Kampani tingta baraknin mukuntak awa balna kidi, dawak sau ritsni balna, sauni pisni balna yak amput yakwarang balna bani yak, lani kat yamwi duwi yamna karang ki.

Skul kalanin, muih sangnika tanitdaknin balna kidi, gabamnit salahni yak duwa ki, dawak, yamwas danin sip awas karang ki, dawak salahni yak sakki, muih bitik yak diana atnin, ahni kau dawas, baisa bitar dawak apahni yaman atnin. Adiak warkni yamwa uni dawak dini balna, gabamint dini kidi, sip tingni dakyakwas karang.

Muih miniknakunni muihni balna yak muihni nuhni yamnini kidi minit dis duna karang, yaka pas yak baisa parasna karang, itang muihbin dunin tingta balna yak. Gabamint yakalauwi muih muih muihni nuhni dawak skul tingta yamwa balna kidi, nining baisa apahni dawak diparaswa karang. Ma muihki nuhni yamnini dawak skul tingta yak, kampani uk balna tingta yamnin ramhni kalawa ki.

Gabamint salahni yak sak ki, pa muihni di yus munwa dini bitik kidi, minit talwak yamni atnin, kaput bik, muih balna bulah duwi minit nuhni ilnin awas yulni tanitdakwa karang ki.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA II

DI DAHNIN TINGNITA

Lanibin 106.

Gabamint di dahnin tingnita dulauwa kidika munah kuduh, pa muihni balna sau dawan kalnin dawi barangni lani kat sau sahyakwi diawa tani duduwa kidi, kaput bik, King sauni pas akat sau sangnika dulaunin warkni dawi, sau baraknin tingta yamnin tani palni duwa ki. Lanibin 180.

Lanibin 181.

Atlantik Kus kau Alas Yalahwa Lani Gabamintni Tanituna walwi yaknin yamwi yayakwa kidi, parasni duwa kidi disna karang ki, La kau ais yamwa munah disnin sip ki tada ulwi dawarang yakarak.

LA TUNA SAHYAKNA III

LA RIKNI SAT UK WIRLANIN

Lanibin 191.

King Sauni Lani Ulwa Anipis kidi adika Lani Rikni pisni as kau sat uk wirlanin parasni duwa ki, dawi Lani Rikni aiska satuk wirlanin tunan bahwa lani ridi laihwi talwi baangni yamna atnin.

Lani Rikni pisni as kau sat uk wirlanin tunan bahwa lani kidika, Tatuna nuhni King tingni yak sakki, awaskat, La yamyang bitik paskaupak tarhti pursin bunitu karak sip karang yamna atnin, La Rikni aiska sat uk wirlanin tunan bahnin lani kalaih, La yamyang bitik pas kaupak, papusdak minitkau as karak sip karang.

Lanibin 192.

La Rikni pisni as sat uk wirlanin tunan bahnin lani kidika angdika lanibin awaskat lani binina wirlani awa kidi laih pakna atnin ki, dawak ais yulni wirlawa tannika bik ulwi lawa atnin ki; dawak kuminti as kau lana karang, dawak ma muihas luih bas pasyakat lani as ulwi lana karang. Lani pisni as satuk wirlanin walwa kidi tanit yak, La ampat ulwi yayakwa tani kat awi yayamna karang.

La Rikni pisni as wirlanin lani kidika kurih bu la yamwa tingnitapasyakat laihwi tatalna karang.

Lanibin 193.

La Rikni aiska sat uk wirlanin tunan bahnin lani kidika, lanibin bayakna kau yamnin yulwa tani balna munah yamna karang, niningkanin dawi lani ulwi yaknin tani balna yakat.

La Rikni aiska sat uk wirlanin tunan bahnin lani kidika barangni yayamna mani, King Sauni Lani Ulwa Anipis yaklauwi mani as pakna karang ki, walwi yaknin yamwi King Sauni Lani Yamyang balna wisamni uk walwi yaknin yulni. King Sauni Lani Yamyang balna kidi parasnina ban duduwa karang, La yamyang wisamni walwi yakwi ahawarang mani kat.

La Rikni wisamni kidi, La yamyang wisamni balna yaklauwi yulwi talwi barangni yayamwas kat, La Rikni umani kidi ban parasni duwa karang ki.

Lanibin 194.

La Rikni pisni as sat uk wirlanin yakat, La yamyang bitik paskaupak siksti pursint bunitu nit karang ki. La Rikni aiska sat uk wirlanin tunan bahnin lani barangni yamnin laih, bitik paskaupak, papusdak minitkau bunitu nit karang ki. Tatuna nuhni King kidi yaklauwi, la Rikni Pisni as satuk wirlawa kidi parasni kalana karang. Akat laih, laihwatnin ramhni duwa kidi ramhni diswa ki.

Lanibin 195.

La Rikni lani balna sat uk wirlanin kidika, la Rikni pisni as sat uk wirlanin yak ampat yamnin tani yuyulwa yaka munah yayamna karang, La yamnin warkni kurih bu yamnin yulwa kidika manah kapat yayamwas karang.
